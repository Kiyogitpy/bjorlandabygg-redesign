# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (chosen in grilling; no build step).

## Users

Homeowners in the Torslanda / Björlanda area of Gothenburg deciding whether to hire a small, trusted builder for house work. (Inferred from the live site; audience was not interviewed in depth.)

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Björlanda Bygg AB, a small Swedish construction company with 35 years of experience "from idea to finished work". Success: a visitor browses the work, trusts the person behind it, and phones Patrik Pettersson.

## Positioning

"Det personliga byggföretaget": one named builder, a local address and a direct phone number, not an anonymous contractor.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two pages: Home and Kontakt. Contact page has a form (Namn, Telefon, E-post, Meddelande) with no backend; the static build uses a mailto fallback.
- Service areas, verbatim from the live site. Utomhus: Fönster, Fasader, Altaner, Uterum, Staket, Tillbyggnader. Inomhus: Badrum, Kök, Tvättstugor, Parkettgolv, Trappor, Lister.
- Swedish is the source language; English is a translation by the assistant and needs native review.
- Undecided: the company's real Instagram URL (not in the scrape).

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing logo (blue hand-lettered "Björlanda Bygg AB" under a drawn roofline) and its blue; the red cabin on blue sky is the existing hero motif. Keep Swedish copy unchanged.

## Evidence on Hand

Real photos only, in ~/bjorlandabygg_scrape/images: two company vans, a tiled-roof house, two finished kitchens, the cabin hero. No testimonials, prices, certifications or project names exist; none may be invented.
Contact: Björlanda Bygg AB, Friluftsvägen 2A, 423 63 Torslanda; Patrik Pettersson, 0708-92 94 94, patrik@bjorlandabygg.se.

## Product Principles

1. Show real work before talking about the company.
2. One person, one phone number, always reachable.
3. Never invent claims, clients or credentials.
4. Plain Swedish first; English is a courtesy, not a separate site.
